Ward and LGA boundaries: GRID3 (Geo-Referenced Infrastructure and Demographic Data for Development), CC BY-SA 4.0

Source: GRID3 NGA Operational Wards v4.0, Lagos State
https://data.humdata.org/dataset/grid3-nga-operational-wards-v4-0

Outage uses these boundaries to list the 20 Lagos local government areas and their 376 wards, and to assign each
H3 resolution-8 hexagon to the ward that contains its centre. The derived files (lagos-places.json, lagos-geo.json)
are adapted from the source (names tidied, outlines simplified, hexagons added; see tools/places/build_lagos.py)
and are shared under the same licence.
