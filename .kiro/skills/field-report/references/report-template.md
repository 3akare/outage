# Report template

Save as `docs/field/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. The checker requires the first line, the two header lines, and the eight sections in this order.

```markdown
# Field report YYYY-MM-DD
Gate status (milestone 1): <met | not met | not measurable yet> (<one-line reason with numbers>)
Kill criterion: <triggered | not triggered | not measurable yet>

Data: <N> opted-in devices, <H> charging hours, generated by <tools/field generator | manual from references/metrics.md>.

## Devices
| Alias | Maker | Android | Mode | Charging h | Armed ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | tecno | 13 | normal | 9.5 | 0.62 |

## Gates
| Check | Value | Threshold | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Armed ratio, best device | 0.97 | at least 0.90 | pass |
| Scheduling delay p95, all devices | 41 s (n=24) | at most 60 s | pass |
| Tecno or Infinix works after exemption | not measured | armed at least 0.90 after exemption | not measurable yet |

## Metrics by class
Class = maker and Android major. Station mode is its own class.
| Class | Devices | Armed ratio | Gaps (count, p50, p95 s) by reason | Delay p50 / p95 s (job) | Delay p50 / p95 s (receiver) | Replug to ring p50 / p95 s | Prompts per day | Alarm rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Outages
| Start (UTC) | Level | Scope | Drops of armed | Labels yes / no / none | Class |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## False alarms and misses
- False alarms: <count>. <one line each, no identifiers>
- Misses: <count>. <armed phones in scope at the time>

## New failures
- `<SIGNATURE>`: `.kiro/specs/bugfix-YYYYMMDD-<slug>` (<count so far>)
- Known signatures seen again: <list with counts>

## Fixture candidates
- <day>, <device count> devices. Export: <command>. Needs the sanitizer and the owner's approval.

## Parameters in use
W = 90 s; Possible: k at least 2 and p below 0.05; Confirmed: k at least 3 and p below 0.001; weight cut 0.5; prior 1 per 1,800 s; restore p95 prior 60 s (values read from `params.ts`, not retyped from memory).
```

Notes:
- Times in UTC. Alias numbers are per request and carry no meaning between days.
- Write "not enough data" instead of a number when the sample is too small.
- Do not paste raw JSON.
