## Notes for the maintainer

- The Samsung page gives menu paths for Android 11, Pie and older and shows the sleeping-app lists in screenshots. Its Android 14 section talks about extra Samsung apps (Good Guardians, Good Lock) and not a settings path. The path "Battery and device care, Battery, Background usage limits" in our entry is not in that source; it is the expected route on recent One UI and must be checked on a phone before the entry is verified.
- The Tecno page describes the manager as one that can freeze apps "completely halted" until the user returns to the app, which is the failure mode the 12-hour armed-time check is meant to catch.
- The Android exemption is checked in code with `PowerManager.isIgnoringBatteryOptimizations`. Direct requests (`ACTION_REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`) are restricted for Play Store apps, and allowed for a sideloaded APK; the app opens the settings list when the direct request is not available.
- Manufacturer strings: we expect Tecno, Infinix and itel phones to report their own names in `Build.MANUFACTURER` and `Build.BRAND`, but no source read here confirms it and it has not been checked on a device. The probe logs `maker` and `brand` for exactly this reason (feasibility-probe R1); fix the `match` tokens if a phone reports something else.
