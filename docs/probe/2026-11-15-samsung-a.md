## Diagnosis

1. **Timeout backoff, not the sensor idea, destroyed armed time.** All 10 job runs ended in `STOP_REASON_TIMEOUT` (reason 3) after ~600 s, which is Android's ~10-minute job limit (expected; https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/job/JobScheduler). The probe's `onStopJob` returned `true`, which asks the system to reschedule using the job's backoff policy. The default is 30 s **exponential** (`retry = stopTime + 30000 * 2^(n-1)`, https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/job/JobInfo.Builder#setBackoffCriteria and https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/job/JobInfo#BACKOFF_POLICY_EXPONENTIAL). The restart gaps are ~50, 61, 142, 241, 515, 986, 1963, 3989 s: each about double the last. That is the exponential backoff, and it contradicts ADR 0001 ("reschedule immediately on every non-charging stop"). Once the gap reached ~1 h the next charge sessions saw no job before power ended, so the last ~3.7 h of charging were fully unarmed. Fixed in task 2 of `work/probe-immediate-reschedule`: return `false` from `onStopJob` and schedule a fresh job immediately, so no backoff accrues.

2. **Arming after plug-in is slow on this phone too.** The library pairs 0 of 5 plug-ins to a job start within the 10-minute window. The two plug-ins that did eventually arm took ~1708 s (~28 min) and ~1009 s (~17 min) from `PLUG CONNECTED` to `JOB_START`, both past the 10-minute pairing limit, so they count as "missed", not as M2 delays. This echoes the ~16-minute arming delay on the Android 15 emulator (ADR 0013 interim findings): a cut soon after a plug-in can be invisible to the job sensor. The backoff fix does not address this arming delay; it is a separate risk for Station mode (ADR 0004) and for the M2 p95 <= 60 s gate, and needs its own measurement on the rerun.

3. **Unplugs were never seen by the job** (0 of 5 paired), because the job was not running at any of the 5 unplugs. The reference receiver saw every plug change (9 of 10 plug states settled within 1 s of the broadcast; 0 of 10 stop-time re-reads differed). Station mode's receiver path (ADR 0004) stays the reliable observer.

4. **Expedited job with the charging constraint is refused** (`IllegalArgumentException`), as documented: expedited jobs may only set network, storage-not-low and persistence constraints (https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/job/JobInfo.Builder#setExpedited(boolean)). A known limitation, not a device fault; it matches the emulator run and rules out ADR 0001 alternative 2.

5. **No mismatch** between the sticky battery intent and BatteryManager at any stop (0 of 10), and no reboots or app restarts in the run.
